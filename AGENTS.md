# Agent rules for ember-oak-coffee-preview

This is a private preview site for a prospective client, Ember & Oak Coffee Co. It is not live.

- Agents never push to main.
- Always open a pull request for Jalen to review and merge. Do not merge it yourself unless Jalen has said you may merge, in his own words. An agent's approval does not count as his.
- Never commit secrets, passwords, or API keys.
- Do not publish this preview publicly — no GitHub Pages, no Netlify or other public hosting, and do not make the repository public — without Jalen's OK in his own words.
- Do not contact the client, reply to her, or buy a domain (or anything else) without Jalen's OK in his own words.
- Keep clearly marked placeholders for anything she has not provided. Do not invent prices, a real phone or email, parking details, reviews, or photos of other businesses.
- Keep agency branding off the public-facing pages (the footer credit stays empty unless Jalen asks for one).
- Times are America/Chicago.
