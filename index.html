The shop

A neighborhood shop, roasted in-house

Ember & Oak Coffee Co. opened last spring at 214 Maple Street, next to the hardware store. We roast our own beans in small batches and serve espresso drinks, pour-overs, loose-leaf tea, and fresh pastries from a local bakery. There are about 30 seats, free Wi-Fi, and a back room that small groups can reserve.

  • About 30 seats
  • Free Wi-Fi
